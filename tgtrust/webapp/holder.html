What this page sends
  • To the issuer: Telegram's signed init data and a blinded request. The request reveals nothing about the credential or the agent.
  • The issuer answers with a blind signature. This page removes the blinding and checks the result twice.
  • If you let this page register a new agent, it also sends the finished credential and the agent's public key. Registering from here right after issuance lets the service guess the link by timing; for strict anonymity use your own key and register later from the agent.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere else. The private key and the blinding secret never leave this device.
